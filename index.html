    How this works

    Install iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu → Install app. Open DirtCalc from that icon.

    First open Tap once. Allow location. Allow motion & orientation once for heading.

    Benchmark Stand on the hub and face the way you will pull. Tap MARK. That locks the bearing the phone is facing, closes the padlock, and stores GPS ALT as the benchmark elevation.

    Mount Fix the phone in the cab with the top of the phone pointing forward. Sit the machine on level ground and tap ZERO. That stores the mount angle.

    Pitch / roll After ZERO, pitch is the grade you are climbing or descending, in percent. Roll is the side tilt, in percent. The incline arrow follows pitch. The pitch card lights when pitch is within ±5% of the target slope, the same window as the compass. SLOPE on the left is still GPS rise/run from the benchmark.

    Grade Drive the line. DIST is run in feet. Δ ELEV is rise from the benchmark. BRG stays on the bearing locked at MARK. The compass ring and HDG light when heading matches that locked bearing within ±5°. The hub and shots stay on the phone if the app restarts.

    Target Tap the slope field for the number pad. 0 is level. ± flips uphill and downhill. CUT/FILL is how far GPS says you are above (CUT) or below (FILL) that plane from the benchmark.

    SHOT Stores this spot against the locked benchmark, plus the cab pitch and roll at that instant.

    Unlock Tap the padlock. Confirm before the benchmark and shots are cleared.

    Phone GPS is a rough helper, not survey or machine control. Typical error is several feet. Do not build final grade from this app.
